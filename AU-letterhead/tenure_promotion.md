The Promotion and Tenure Committee of the Department of Information Systems \&
Security (ISS) has examined the record of Assistant Professor Dr. Jason
Williams, who has been on the tenure track at Augusta University since August
2020. Dr. Williams’ workload allocation has emphasized research, with 55%
research, 35% teaching, and 10% service.

The Committee carefully evaluated Dr. Williams’ record of accomplishments and
determined that he has met the expectations of the tenure-track position. After
reviewing his contributions in research, teaching, service, student success, and
professional development, the Committee unanimously recommends him for tenure
and promotion.

# Evaluation Rubric

At the time of Dr. William's appointment the school was influx and growing. In
2021, an official Information Systems \& Security Promotion and Tenure Policy
was adopted, but since then several revisions have occurred. Since Dr. Williams'
appointment was closest to the 2021 policy, we as a committee used that years
policy as our rubric for Dr. Williams' evaluation. 

# Teaching / Mentoring

Over the last six years Dr. Williams has taught a number of courses including:
AIST 4720 (Enterprise System Architectures), AIST 6357 (Information Risk
Management), AIST 7110 (Qualitative Research Methods in Information Systems),
AIST 6365 (Information Security Project Management ), and CYBR 2600
(Introduction to Networking and Cyber Security). One point that is clear from
the evidence provided by Dr. Williams is that he has actively pursued
improvements in his pedagogy, classroom management, and lecturing. His course
evaluations, with a mean response rate of 64.2% and mean quantitative scores
between 4 and 5 the last three years and 3 and 5 the first three years of his
tenure, show improvements in all of these areas, and they make clear that he has
continually improved each year. He adds real-world experience by introducing his
students to applications such as Microsoft Azure and SimpleRisk. In addition, he
uses modern teaching methods such as the flipped classroom, debate style group
activities, and project-based learning to enhance the learning experience for
his students. Lastly, all of the above is echoed by his peer reviewer who note
the following in their feedback:

- Dr. Williams demonstrates strong subject-matter expertise and explains
  difficult concepts clearly, using historical context, recent research,
  examples, metaphors, figures, flowcharts, and other visuals effectively.

- Dr. Williams actively engages students through frequent participation,
  varied questioning techniques, discussion, coding exercises, and integration
  of material from previous weeks.

- The classroom environment is participatory and conducive to critical thinking,
  with appropriate pauses and opportunities for group discussion.

- Presentation quality is strong: slides are clear, well structured, easy to
  see, and supported by effective use of projection and visual materials.

- Self-paced/asynchronous course modules are particularly well organized, with
  schedules, instructions, goals, assessments, and milestones clearly
  communicated.

# Research / Scholarship

Dr. Williams meets the standards for outstanding scholarship. The quality of the
his papers are exceptional, rising citations to recent papers are a signal of
impact, and the Dr. Williams' ongoing work suggests a continuation and increase
in research production and impact. He has been awarded $3k in internal grants
with AU, and have several proposals under review. Based on the current evidence
we believe Dr. Williams' research will grow and he will eventually secure
external funding. Furthermore, Dr. Williams has served on many program
committees to conferences/workshops and as a referee to several journals.

The quality of Dr. Williams journal publications, 6 since 2022, is noteworthy.
Based on the ABDC journal rating 4/6 papers are in A venues, 1 in a C venue, and
1 in a SCImago rated 2 venue. Furthermore, his two latest publications from 2025
already have 5 or more citations. This is an uncommon occurrence. It typically
takes more time for papers to begin accumulating citations. This shows that he
is targeting high-quality venues and publishing research that is of interest to
the wider Information Systems community.  Dr. Williams has also published at
various conferences (~14 contributions) showing a high-level of engagement with
the research community. Lastly, he has several works in progress which when
combined with his past record shows that he will continue with is current
research momentum.

Finally, Dr. Williams has shown a commitment to student mentorship by mentoring
a PhD student, working with other graduate research assistants at both the PhD
and master's level, and working with undergraduate research assistants. Several
of his projects with students have resulted in publications.

# Student Success Activities

Dr. Williams has shown a true to commitment to student success that should be
commended. He has been advising a PhD student, Ron Martin, resulting in
publications as well as mentoring him through a successful application to the
ACM SIGMIS CPR 2026 Doctoral Consortium. He also served on the doctoral
committee for another PhD student, Tal Lavi. Dr. Williams also proved a
three-semester research internship to a graduate student that resulted in
co-authored publication.  Finally, he has worked with a large number of
undergraduate students, as well as being an active member of the Undergraduate
Honors Committee. 

A noteworthy contribution is his inclusion of students in real research. He
included IRB-approved research projects in his AIST 7110 (Qualitative Research
Course) that resulted in a join publication. In addition, he received a $2k
Education Innovation Fund (EIF) grant to provide his students with the necessary
credits to make use of the Microsoft Azure platform.  

Dr. Williams has served on the AU General Scholarship Review Committee
(2023–2025), mentored fellow colleagues through their first-time teaching
duties, as well as presented at local high schools. This is a direct
contribution to service and partnerships for student support.

# Letters of External Reviewers

In total, 9 external reviewers submitted letters that unanimously agree that Dr.
Williams is an accomplished scholar and teacher. Every reviewer highlighted the
strength of Dr. Williams' journal publications and commitment to teaching and
professional development through innovative methods and continuing education.

The following are summaries of the noteworthy points made by each external
reviewer:

1. This letter made it clear that Dr. Williams has developed a coherent and
   productive research program with a clear scholarly identity focused on how
   individuals and organizations use, misuse, evaluate, and respond to
   information technologies. In addition, the reviewer states that Dr. Williams'
   research addresses topical, theoretically meaningful, and socially
   consequential challenges that are increasingly important to organizations and
   society. 
   
2. This letter notes that Dr. Williams’s file is strong, and his promotion and
   tenure are supported without reservation. Then go on to say that his
   publications and research activities form a coherent program rather than a
   collection of unrelated projects, and his citation trajectory is encouraging,
   increasing from single-digit annual citations early in his career to more
   than 20 per year by 2025, suggesting growing recognition of his recent work. 

3. This letter reinforces the others by saying that Dr. Williams has developed a
   strong, active, and coherent research program characterized by sustained
   productivity, continued engagement with the Information Systems community,
   and a healthy research pipeline. They point out that a particular strength is
   his focus on developing and evaluating interventions that address
   consequential problems in cybersecurity, information quality, and
   decision-making, connecting rigorous scholarship with issues of importance to
   organizations and society and positioning his work to contribute to both
   research and practice.

4. The reviewer of this letter was impressed by Dr. Williams' research record
   for his career stage. They state that it appears well positioned for
   sustained success as a researcher, and  his recent productivity, strong
   research pipeline, and invited presentations demonstrate both continuing
   scholarly activity and recognized expertise within his research stream.

5. This reviewer echos the previous writers, but points out that Dr. Williams'
   19 conference presentations and proceedings further demonstrate extensive
   engagement with the field and active development of his research program.

6. This letter highlights that collectively, Dr. Williams' research moves beyond
   technical solutions to examine how individuals interpret, evaluate, and act
   on information, establishing him as an agile scholar addressing important
   IT-related issues with research quality appropriate to his publication
   venues.

7. This letter emphasizes that Dr. Williams' scholarship demonstrates both
   sustained productivity and intellectual development, while its growing
   influence beyond his home institution indicates an expanding professional
   reputation and trajectory.

8. This letter highlights that Dr. Williams' authorship record demonstrates that
   he drives many of his research projects, while his clearly defined and
   interconnected research streams provide evidence of a coherent agenda and a
   strong foundation for continued productivity.

9. Finally, this letter points out that Dr. Williams has an impressive
   publication record, his Best Paper Award nominations, competitive acceptance
   at selective conferences, ambitious grant-seeking efforts, and
   interdisciplinary collaborations further strengthen his record. Taken
   together, these accomplishments demonstrate a distinct and accelerating
   research trajectory that compares favorably with successful tenure candidates
   in Information Systems and meets the SCCS standard for outstanding
   scholarship.
  
All letter writers state that Dr. Williams has a strong commitment to teaching
excellence. This is demonstrated through instructional innovation, the use of
evidence-based pedagogy, and his continued education. Through the noticeable
improvements made throughout his years teaching it is clear he cares deeply about
his students success.
